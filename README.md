# Salesforce CRM Plugin for Claude

Connect your Salesforce CRM to Claude. No coding required.

## How to Use

### 1. Download & Install

Download **[salesforce-crm.plugin](./salesforce-crm.plugin)** and double-click to install in Claude.

### 2. Connect Your Account

Open a new chat in Claude and say:

> **"I want to connect my Salesforce account"**

Claude will ask for your login details one by one:
- Your Salesforce email
- Your password
- Your Security Token (Claude will explain how to get it)

Claude handles everything else automatically. No terminal, no code.

### 3. Restart Claude

After Claude sets things up, just **quit Claude (Cmd+Q)** and reopen it. Done!

## What You Can Do

Just chat naturally:
- "Show me my recent leads"
- "Create a new lead for John Smith at Acme Corp"
- "Search opportunities closing this month"
- "Log a call with the marketing team"
- "Run a SOQL query to find all contacts at Google"

## Troubleshooting

- **Not connecting?** Make sure you restarted Claude after setup. On some Macs, a full reboot helps.
- **Wrong credentials?** Say "I want to reconnect my Salesforce" and Claude will update your details.
